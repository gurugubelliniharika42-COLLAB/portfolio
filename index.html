What I've built

Major project

VetNet

A study platform for veterinary students

VetNet brings subjects, notes, practice and planning into one single-page website. I built it with only three files (index.html, a CSS file and a JavaScript file), with no frameworks, and I deployed it on Vercel and GitHub Pages.

The idea came from a veterinary student I know. She told me how difficult it was to find study resources in one place. I wasn't familiar with veterinary education myself, so I had to understand what students in that course actually need before I could build anything useful.

I chose them as my audience because the problem was real and I could see a way to help. That is how VetNet started.

  • MCQ quiz with 10, 20, 40 or all questions.
  • Flashcards that flip when you tap them.
  • Exam practice in six different formats.
  • Glossary for quick lookup of terms.
  • Study planner with All, Pending and Completed filters.
  • Notebook, bookmarks and progress tracking saved in the browser, so students continue where they stopped.
  • Internship Learning Centre with a case history template, an abbreviation table and a revision checklist for clinical rotations.
  • Easy to use on a phone, with a mobile-friendly menu and a skip-to-content link.

The hardest part was organizing the content as the number of subjects and topics grew. I wanted the site to stay simple to move around in, even with more material added.

I worked on the structure and arranged the materials subject-wise. It took a few rounds of changes, but it taught me something I still follow: plan the structure first, and add features and content after that.

Students who explored VetNet gave positive feedback. They said it was very useful, mainly because the study materials are organized in one place, which was the whole point of the project.

What I'm proudest of is how it grew. It started as a small website and slowly became a learning platform with multiple subjects and organized study material. Hearing that it helped gave me more motivation to keep improving it.
